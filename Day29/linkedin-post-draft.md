Day 29/60 — Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab 🚨📦

Built an interactive crisis simulator today — you take over a random company, get hit with a random supply chain disaster (factory fire, port strike, cyberattack), and make real leadership decisions across 6 stages: War Room response, supplier negotiation, CEO boardroom, and AI strategy.

The part I actually learned the most from wasn't the UI — it was the scoring model underneath it.

The final "Crisis Score" is a weighted roll-up of 6 sub-metrics (Leadership, Negotiation, Resilience, Cost Control, Risk Management, Customer Satisfaction) — the exact same pattern behind real-world scorecards: credit risk models, vendor risk indices, performance ratings.

Turns out designing a good "why does this matter" explanation for every decision is harder than writing the decision logic itself. If a beginner can't tell why an option matters, the choice feels random — even when the math behind it isn't.

Next up: rebuilding this same weighted-KPI model in Power BI with DAX measures. Same logic, analyst-native tool.

Stack: React (CDN) + Babel JSX, single HTML file, zero backend, fully offline.
