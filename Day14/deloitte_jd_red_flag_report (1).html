Top Red Flags
8/10
MA/MS/MBA required for an entry-level analyst role
The JD lists a postgraduate degree as mandatory alongside a bachelor's — unusual for an "Analyst" title. This is either a copy-paste error or signals the role is more senior than advertised.
7/10
No salary or compensation information
Zero mention of CTC, pay band, or even a range. Big 4 India roles are known for below-market base pay, especially in CoRe (support) functions vs. consulting. Negotiate hard or ask directly.
6/10
Broad scope with too many concurrent tools
Expects Tableau, Tableau Prep, Adobe Analytics, MS Excel + Macros, VBA, ETL tools, and web traffic tools — all for one analyst. That's a wide tech surface for a single role with 1–3 years experience.
5/10
CoRe (internal support) vs. client-facing consulting
This role is under "CoRe Business Solutions" — Deloitte's internal services arm, not client-facing consulting. Career growth, learning exposure, and prestige differ significantly from Deloitte Consulting roles.
4/10
Work timing: 11am–8pm
Aligns with US time zones, suggesting this team supports global (likely US) stakeholders. Weekend or extended hours during reporting cycles are common in such setups.
3/10
Vague growth language ("no two people learn in the same way")
The "How you'll grow" section is boilerplate with no concrete promotion timelines, mentorship structure, or data on internal mobility rates.
Positive Signals
Brand prestige — Deloitte is a globally recognized Big 4 firm. The name alone adds significant value to your resume, especially early-career.
Structured learning — DU: The Leadership Center in India (Hyderabad) is a real, world-class facility with structured L&D programs.
Clear technical expectations — The JD is specific about tools (Tableau, Adobe Analytics, Excel/VBA), making it easier to self-assess and prep.
Realistic experience ask — 1–3 years overall experience is reasonable for an Analyst role and opens it to fresh graduates with internships.
Global exposure — Supporting Fortune 500 clients across geographies is genuine skill-building, even in a CoRe function.
Stable company — Deloitte's $70B+ revenue and 470K workforce make layoff risk very low at the analyst level.

5 Smart Interview Questions
Question 1 — Salary clarity
"What is the compensation band for this role, and how does it compare to Deloitte's consulting analyst tracks?"
Question 2 — MBA requirement
"The JD lists an MA/MS/MBA as a requirement — is this strictly necessary, or is strong technical experience and a B.Tech considered equivalent?"
Question 3 — CoRe vs. consulting mobility
"Are there structured pathways for CoRe Analytics professionals to transition into Deloitte's client-facing consulting practice?"
Question 4 — Tool depth vs. breadth
"Of the tools listed — Tableau, Adobe Analytics, VBA, ETL — which are used daily vs. occasionally? I want to prioritise where to deepen expertise."
Question 5 — Work timing & expectations
"The 11am–8pm shift suggests global stakeholder alignment. How often does this extend during reporting periods, and is there flexibility around that window?"
