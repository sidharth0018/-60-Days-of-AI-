# Day 25/60 - AI Shark Tank Simulator 🦈

## Challenge Overview

Today I designed an AI-powered Shark Tank Simulator using Claude AI.

The goal was to create a complete startup evaluation platform where founders can pitch their ideas and receive feedback from multiple AI investors, similar to the real Shark Tank experience.

---

## Problem Statement

Many aspiring entrepreneurs struggle to validate startup ideas before approaching investors.

Traditional startup evaluation requires:
- Industry experts
- Mentors
- Investors
- Market validation

which are often difficult to access.

---

## Solution

An AI-powered Shark Tank Simulator that acts as a virtual investment panel.

Users enter:

- Startup Name
- Problem Statement
- Solution
- Revenue Model
- Target Audience
- Funding Ask

The AI evaluates the startup from multiple perspectives and generates an investment decision.

---

## Key Features

### 1. AI Investor Panel

Four AI judges evaluate every startup.

#### Venture Capitalist
Focus:
- Market size
- Scalability
- Growth potential

#### Startup Founder
Focus:
- Execution
- Team capability
- Product development

#### Customer Representative
Focus:
- User pain points
- Adoption
- Customer experience

#### Angel Investor
Focus:
- ROI
- Financial viability
- Risk analysis

---

### 2. Interactive Pitch Round

- Founder submits startup pitch
- Each judge asks 2 questions
- User responds
- AI generates dynamic reactions

---

### 3. Startup Scoring System

Each startup receives scores out of 100.

| Category | Weight |
|-----------|---------|
| Market Potential | 20 |
| Innovation | 20 |
| Business Model | 20 |
| Execution Capability | 20 |
| Investment Worthiness | 20 |

---

### 4. Investment Decisions

Possible outcomes:

- INVEST
- REJECT
- ACQUIRE
- COME BACK LATER

---

### 5. Funding Analysis

AI generates:

- Suggested valuation
- Recommended investment amount
- Equity percentage
- Risk assessment

---

## Startup Tested

### FarmEasy 🚜

#### Problem

Agricultural equipment such as tractors, harvesters, and rotavators are expensive for small and marginal farmers.

#### Solution

Farmers can rent agricultural machinery on-demand at affordable prices instead of purchasing costly equipment.

#### Revenue Model

Commission-based equipment rental marketplace.

#### Target Audience

Small and medium-scale farmers.

#### Funding Ask

₹50 Lakhs for platform development and market expansion.
