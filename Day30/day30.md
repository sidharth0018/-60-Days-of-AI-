# 🚚 Day 30 - Supply Chain Builder

> Part of my **60 Day Claude AI Challenge**

## 📌 Overview

Today I challenged Claude AI to generate a complete educational web application that teaches Supply Chain Management through an interactive simulation.

Instead of creating a static webpage, the goal was to build an application where users make real business decisions and instantly see how those decisions impact an entire supply chain.

The result is **Supply Chain Builder**, a single-file React application that explains supply chain concepts in beginner-friendly language while allowing users to build their own supply chain from scratch.

---

# 🎯 Challenge Goal

Build an educational simulator that teaches:

- What a supply chain is
- How businesses design supply chains
- Trade-offs behind business decisions
- Cost vs Speed vs Risk
- Inventory Management
- Transportation Selection
- Supplier Strategy
- Warehouse Planning

---

# 📊 Live Business Metrics

The application updates five metrics after every decision.

| Metric | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Cost | Overall operating cost |
| Delivery Speed | Customer delivery performance |
| Risk | Supply chain vulnerability |
| Customer Satisfaction | Customer experience |
| Sustainability | Environmental impact |

Each decision immediately affects these metrics.

# 📈 Final Dashboard

After completing the simulation, users receive:
