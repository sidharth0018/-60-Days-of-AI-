# Technical Notes

## Architecture

App

├── Welcome Screen

├── Company Generator

├── Decision Components

├── Live Metrics

├── Dashboard

└── Replay

---

## State Management

React useState manages:

- Current Step
- Company Data
- User Decisions
- Business Metrics
- Final Score

---

## Business Logic

Each decision modifies:

- Cost
- Delivery Speed
- Risk
- Sustainability
- Customer Satisfaction

The final score is calculated from all business metrics.
