## PROMPT 1 — Hyperrealistic Data Analyst Scene
## Best Tools: Adobe Firefly, Midjourney v6

A young Indian male data analyst in his mid-20s seated at a sleek
dark-wood desk in a modern tech office at golden hour, three ultra-wide
monitors displaying glowing dashboards with bar charts, heatmaps, and
Python code, warm amber light streaming through floor-to-ceiling windows
casting long soft shadows, bokeh background of an open-plan office with
blurred colleagues and city skyline through glass, shallow depth of field
with sharp focus on his concentrated expression and fingertips on keyboard,
muted earth tones with electric blue and amber accent glows from screens,
photorealistic, 8K resolution, 16:9 aspect ratio, shot style of a
professional tech company editorial photograph, 300 DPI

Dimensions breakdown:
- Subject: Indian male data analyst at desk
- Style: Photorealistic, editorial photograph
- Color Palette: Muted earth + electric blue + amber screen glow
- Lighting: Golden hour, floor-to-ceiling windows, soft shadows
- Composition: Shallow depth of field, subject at center, bokeh bg
- Resolution: 8K, 16:9, 300 DPI
- Constraints: No text on screens readable, no faces of other people
- Detail: Micro-details on keyboard, screen reflections in eyes

depth of field from foreground data particles to infinite background stars,
no text, painterly yet futuristic, 7680x4320 PNG, 16:9, high contrast

Dimensions breakdown:

- Color Palette: Electric blue, violet, gold on deep space black

- Composition: Person at center, lattice radiates outward

## PROMPT 3 — Career Pivot Motivational Scene
## Best Tools: DALL-E 3, Midjourney

A wide-angle photorealistic scene of a young professional walking
confidently on a glass bridge suspended between two skyscrapers at dawn,
one tower representing ECE/electronics (circuit boards, antenna motifs),
the other glowing with data science iconography (graphs, code, BI dashboards),
soft sunrise light in rose gold and cyan, misty clouds below,
city skyline visible in background, rule-of-thirds composition with the
figure at the left third looking toward the data science tower,
cinematic depth, motivational yet grounded mood,
4K digital illustration, 16:9, no explicit text on buildings

Dimensions breakdown:
- Subject: Young professional on glass bridge between two career towers
- Style: Photorealistic digital illustration
- Color Palette: Rose gold + cyan sunrise, misty atmospheric
- Lighting: Dawn, directional sunrise from right, soft shadows
- Composition: Rule of thirds, figure at left third, towers frame image
- Resolution: 4K, 16:9
- Constraints: No readable text, no faces clearly visible
- Detail: Glass bridge texture, misty cloud depth, tower details

## THE 8-DIMENSION FRAMEWORK (Template for any image prompt)

[SUBJECT: what is depicted, who/what is the focal point]
[SETTING: environment, location, time period]
[STYLE: photorealistic / hyperrealistic / digital art / watercolor / oil painting]
[COLOR PALETTE: dominant hues, temperature (warm/cool), mood]
[LIGHTING: time of day, light source direction, quality (hard/soft), shadows]
[COMPOSITION: focal point, rule of thirds, depth of field, angle]
[RESOLUTION: dimensions, aspect ratio, DPI, file format]
[CONSTRAINTS: include X, avoid Y, aspect ratio locks]
[DETAIL LEVEL: texture fidelity, micro-details, realism vs painterly balance]
