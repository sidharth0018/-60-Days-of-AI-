How this was researched

Approach

Cost and duration were pulled from official provider pages and Coursera's own program listings where available, cross-checked against independent review sites for consistency. Job/placement support, curriculum depth, and industry recognition are harder to quantify objectively, so those three scores are analyst judgments (0–10 scale) informed by the sourced facts below — not third-party benchmark numbers — and are flagged as estimates in each card.

Conflicts resolved

  • Google certificate pricing varies by source ($49/mo per most reviews vs $59/mo on one Coursera listing, likely a regional or Plus-bundle price). Used the more frequently cited $49/month standalone rate and Coursera's own 6-month median pace ($294 total) as the primary figure.
  • IBM certificate length is reported as 8, 9, or 11 courses depending on the source and update date — Coursera has been revising the curriculum through 2026. Used the most recent (Feb 2026) figure of an 11-course series, noting older sources say 8–9.
  • upGrad pricing spans a wide range (₹80,000–₹1,50,000+) across upGrad's own multiple data programs (Advanced Certificate vs Executive PG vs M.Sc.). Used the Advanced Certificate Program in Data Analytics (₹99,999) since it's the closest match to the other three "certificate," not "degree," options.
  • DataCamp has no single "career track" certificate the way the others do — it's subscription access to modular tracks plus an optional certification exam. Time and cost figures reflect the subscription model, not a bundled program fee.

What's an estimate

Job/placement support, curriculum depth, and industry recognition scores (shown out of 10 in each card) are the research analyst's synthesis of the cited sources, not numbers published by the providers themselves. Cost and time figures are sourced directly except where noted above.
