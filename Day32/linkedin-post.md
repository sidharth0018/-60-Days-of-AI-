Day 32/60 of my Claude AI challenge, I built a tool to teach marketing strategy — then used it on myself.

Most AI marketing tools generate posts. Mine forces a decision sequence instead: define the audience, pick platforms (each labeled strong fit / weak fit with a reason), commit to exactly 3 content pillars, plan 4 weekly goals, then react to a random curveall — viral post, podcast invite, someone copying your content.

The interesting part wasn't building it. It was running "Build My Personal Brand" mode on myself as a Data Analyst job seeker.

The tool told me my strongest move is leading with Thought Leadership over reach-chasing, and flagged that my instinct to "go deeper when someone copies your style" only works if I'm actually consistent about it.

It also confirmed something I'd been guessing at — LinkedIn should be my primary channel, not a secondary one.

Single-file React app, no backend, runs fully offline. Every step ends with a copy-pasteable prompt so it teaches prompt engineering alongside marketing.
