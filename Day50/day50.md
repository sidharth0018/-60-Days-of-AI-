## Overview

Today's challenge was completely different from traditional interview preparation.

Instead of solving coding questions, I went through a simulated **Data Analyst Technical Interview** where every project, skill, and claim on my resume was questioned in depth.

The interviewer continuously challenged my answers until it was clear whether I genuinely understood my own work.

This experience taught me one important lesson:

> **Your resume gets you the interview.**
>
> **Your understanding gets you the job.**
