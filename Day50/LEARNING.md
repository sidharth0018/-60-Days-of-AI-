# Interview Learnings

## Lesson 1

Never write something on your resume that you cannot explain.

## Lesson 2

Interviewers keep asking "Why?"

Until they reach the limit of your understanding.

---

## Lesson 3

Honesty is better than guessing.

If you don't remember something, explain the concept honestly.
